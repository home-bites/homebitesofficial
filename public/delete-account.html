Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your HomeBites account and the personal information associated with it. This page explains exactly what is removed, what we are required to keep, and how to make the request.

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Two ways to delete your account

Fastest — immediate

Delete in the HomeBites app

Deletion happens straight away, and you will see confirmation on screen.

  1. Open the HomeBites app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Read the summary, then type DELETE PERMANENTLY to confirm
No app needed

Request deletion from this page

Use this if you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in. We verify your identity against the account before deleting anything.

  1. Complete the form below
  2. We acknowledge your request within 48 hours
  3. We may contact you to confirm you own the account
  4. Deletion is completed within 7 days
You cannot delete your account while an order is in progress. If you have an order that is placed, being prepared, or out for delivery, deletion is blocked until it is delivered or cancelled. This protects both you and the delivery partner carrying your order.

What happens to your data

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. There is no recovery period and no way to restore the account afterwards. If you sign up again later, it will be an entirely new account with no history, saved addresses or balances.

Data What happens
Saved delivery addresses Deleted permanently.
Favourites and saved items Deleted permanently.
Shopping cart Deleted permanently.
Your profile Name, email address, mobile number, profile photo and notification token are deleted. The profile record itself remains only as an empty marker so that past order records stay valid.
Sign-in credentials Deleted permanently. You will no longer be able to log in by any method, including Google sign-in and mobile OTP.
Active subscriptions Cancelled immediately. No further meals are delivered and no further renewal payments are taken.
Wallet, cashback and pending balances Forfeited. Any remaining balance is set to zero and is not refundable. Withdraw or spend your balance before deleting.
Past order history Anonymised, not deleted. Your name, phone number, email address and delivery address are stripped from every past order. What remains is an unidentifiable transaction record.
Your wallet balance is not refunded. Deleting your account forfeits any money remaining in your HomeBites wallet, including cashback and pending amounts. If you have a balance you want to use, spend it before you request deletion.

Why some records are kept

We keep an anonymised record of completed orders because we are required to retain transaction and financial records for the applicable statutory periods under Indian tax and accounting law, and because those records may be needed for fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance. These records no longer identify you: the customer name is replaced with “Deleted user”, and the contact details, delivery address and location coordinates are removed entirely.

We also keep a minimal audit entry recording that a deletion took place and when. This entry stores a one-way cryptographic hash of your mobile number rather than the number itself — enough to demonstrate that we honoured your request, without retaining the identifier the deletion was meant to remove.

For full details of how we handle information, see our Privacy Policy.

How long it takes

  • In-app deletion — immediate.
  • Acknowledgement of a request from this page — within 48 hours.
  • Completion of a request from this page — within 7 days of us verifying that you own the account, and in every case within 30 days of the request.
  • Anonymised transaction records — retained for the applicable statutory period, then deleted or securely disposed of.
  • Backups — deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for a short period before those backups are rotated out. It is not restored to live systems.
